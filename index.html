Our Little
Love Story

A little message from my heart
to the girl I can't stop thinking about.

♡

Dear Julie,

I honestly don't know how to explain what has been happening inside my heart lately.

Ever since our date, I keep thinking about you. I remember the little moments, the conversations, your smile, and the way being with you made everything feel a little more special.

And now that I'm here writing this message, I realized something...

I think I'm falling for you. Hard. ♡

I miss you more than I expected. Sometimes I'm just doing something completely normal, then suddenly my mind goes back to you.

I find myself wanting to talk to you, wanting to know how your day went, wanting to hear your stories, wanting to make you laugh, and wanting to be there whenever you need someone.

And honestly, Julie... I want to tell you that I love you every day.

Not because I expect anything from you, but because that's simply what my heart feels.

I love the way you make ordinary moments feel special. I love your presence. I love your smile. I love the little things about you that you probably don't even realize I notice.

I know we're still writing our story, and I don't want to rush anything. I just want to enjoy every chapter with you, one day at a time.

I miss you, Julie. ♡

And if my heart could speak for itself, it would probably say your name all day.

Thank you for giving me memories that I keep replaying in my head and a reason to smile at my phone.

Whatever happens next, I hope you know that there is someone here who genuinely cares about you, prays for you, supports you, and wants to see you happy.

And yes... that someone is me. ♡

Always thinking of you,
James ♡
